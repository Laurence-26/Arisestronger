The System

AriseStronger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026 · Last updated: 14 September 2026

Short version: AriseStronger is fully offline. We do not collect, sell, share, or upload your personal data. Your Hunter, streak, rank, and workouts stay on your phone unless you export a backup file yourself.

1. Who we are

AriseStronger is a Solo Leveling–inspired daily fitness quest app published from the open-source project at github.com/Laurence-26/Arisestronger. This policy applies to the Android app named AriseStronger.

2. Data we collect

We collect none of the following:

  • Name, email, phone number, or account credentials (there is no account)
  • Location, contacts, photos, or microphone data
  • Advertising IDs, analytics, or crash reports sent to us
  • Payment information

The app does not include a login, cloud sync, ads, or a remote server.

3. Information stored only on your device

To make the quest system work, AriseStronger saves data in a local SQLite database on your device, including:

  • Hunter display names you type
  • Chosen starting rank, total days, streak, and rank progress
  • Custom exercises and daily check-offs
  • Quest history, missed-day warnings, and penalty records
  • Reminder time and notification preference on the device

This information never leaves the device through the app. Uninstalling AriseStronger, or deleting a Hunter inside the app, removes that local data.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, AriseStronger schedules local reminders (a morning quote and an evening incomplete-quest warning). These are delivered by your phone’s notification system. They are not sent through our servers, because we have none.

5. Backups you choose to make

You may export a JSON backup and share it with another app (email, Drive, Files, etc.). That file contains only the Hunter data you already stored locally. We do not receive it. If you share a backup, the service you pick (Google Drive, WhatsApp, and so on) has its own privacy policy.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications — optional, for local daily reminders.
  • Vibrate / boot completed — so scheduled local reminders can fire and return after a reboot.
  • The app is designed to work without internet. It does not require an account.

7. Children

AriseStronger is a general fitness habit tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal data at all, a child’s use of the app still stays on their device.

8. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell data. We do not share data with advertisers, analytics companies, or other third parties, because we do not receive it.

9. Security

Your progress is stored in the app’s private local storage on the device, protected by Android’s normal app sandbox. You are responsible for the security of any backup file you export.

10. Your choices

  • Revoke notification permission in Android settings at any time.
  • Delete a Hunter from the Hunter picker to erase that profile’s local data.
  • Uninstall the app to erase remaining AriseStronger data on that device.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the copy in the GitHub repository. Continued use of a newly installed version means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on github.com/Laurence-26/Arisestronger/issues.
